Privacy policy

Fuel & Burn · Last updated 17 October 2026

In short

  • Your diary stays on your phone. Your profile, weight, body fat, food and activity are saved only on your device. There is no account and no server copy.
  • Only food you ask the app to recognise leaves your device: the text, voice note or photo, sent to an AI service, without your name, weight or anything else about you. Neither the app nor our relay keeps it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete everything at any time with “Delete all my data” in the ⋮ menu.

Who is responsible

Fuel & Burn is a free personal project by Ekaterina Mikhaylova, Barcelona, Spain, who is the data controller for the little personal data that reaches a server. Questions or requests: info@mikhaylova.dev.

What stays on your device

To estimate your burn and show your trend, the app saves on your device, in the browser’s storage for this site:

  • your profile: sex, age, height and activity level;
  • weigh-ins and, if you log them, body fat readings;
  • the food and activity you log, your saved foods, your goal and targets;
  • your settings, such as calm view, and a record of when you agreed to this policy.

Weight, body fat, food and activity are data about your health. They are not sent anywhere, and we cannot see them. The calculations (resting burn, maintenance, trends) all run on your device.

Because the data lives only in this browser, clearing the site’s data, uninstalling the app or losing your phone removes it. “Export backup” saves a copy as a file you choose where to keep; a backup you store in a cloud drive or send to yourself is then under that service’s terms.

What is sent when you use food recognition

Recognition is optional; you can always search the food list or use your saved foods instead. When you tap “Recognize”, “Speak” or “Camera”:

  • Text: what you typed about your meal.
  • Voice: a recording of up to 30 seconds, made only after you tap “Speak”, turned into text and then treated like typed text.
  • Photo: the photo you take or pick, made smaller on your device first (about 768 pixels), with any note you typed about it.

This goes over an encrypted connection to a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers, which passes it to OpenAI’s API and returns a list of foods and amounts. The calories are then looked up on your device in the food table. Nothing about you is sent with it: no name, no weight, no diary.

What is kept: the relay does not store your text, audio or photos. The app does not keep the recording or the photo either. OpenAI processes them on our behalf; under its API terms it does not use them to train its models and may keep them for up to 30 days to detect abuse before deleting them. Please don’t include other people’s faces or personal details in food photos.

What servers see when you open the app

  • Website hosting. The app’s files come from our server at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany through Cloudflare. Like any website, they see your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, kept in server logs for 14 days to keep the site running and secure.
  • Fair-use limit on recognition. To prevent abuse, the relay counts recognition requests per day under a shortened, scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address. The count is deleted automatically after two days and is not linked to anything else.

The app uses no cookies. Its fonts and food table are part of the app, so they are not fetched from other sites.

Why we may use it (legal bases)

  • Your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)) for health data and food recognition, given in the app before you start. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data; this doesn’t affect anything done before.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for server logs and the fair-use limit: keeping the service working, secure and affordable.

Other companies involved

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: delivers the site and runs the recognition relay.
  • OpenAI, L.L.C.: turns food text, voice and photos into a list of foods.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): hosts the app’s files.

Cloudflare and OpenAI are based in the United States, so food sent for recognition and connection data may be processed there. These transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in each provider’s data processing agreement and, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Most of these you can use straight from the app, since your data is on your device:

  • See and take your data: “Export backup” gives you all of it as a JSON file.
  • Correct it: edit your profile, weigh-ins and diary entries in the app.
  • Delete it: “Delete all my data” in the ⋮ menu erases everything on the device.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your data and stop using the app, or simply don’t use food recognition.

You also have the right to object, to restrict processing and to complain to a data protection authority, for example the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or the one where you live. We can’t look up or delete data on your behalf because we never receive it; for anything else, write to info@mikhaylova.dev.

Who it’s for

Fuel & Burn is for adults, 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Not medical advice

Fuel & Burn is a wellness app. Its estimates are approximate and are not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have had difficulties with eating, talk to a doctor before changing how you eat.

Other apps and devices

As a web app, Fuel & Burn can’t read data from other health apps, watches or scales, and doesn’t share data with them. If that changes, for example in an Android version, this policy will say so before it happens.

Security

The app is served only over HTTPS. The key for the AI service stays on the relay and is never part of the app. Your diary is protected by your device’s own lock and the browser’s separation between sites; anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, for example new data leaving your device, the app will tell you and ask again where consent is needed. The date at the top shows the latest version.
